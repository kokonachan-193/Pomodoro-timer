# Aqua Focus v2.1.6

## Highlights

- Selected/custom background images now render during Focus.
- Custom background selection persists across restarts.
- Background dimming, Home UI scale and Focus visual strength are customizable.
- Focus animations are managed as Extensions:
  - Rising Water
  - Deep Light Shafts
  - Depth Particles
- Japanese / English coverage expanded across the clean Home, Settings, Tasks, Stats, Extensions, Music Library, Quick Dive, Countdown, Stopwatch, Alarm and Full Controls additions.
- Release smoke test now explicitly verifies that a custom background is actually rendered on the Focus canvas.
